# The Field Guide to Global Payments — Visual Technical Breakdown

**Reference**: Sophia Goldberg's *The Field Guide to Global Payments* (fieldguidetoglobalpayments.com)  
**Deliverable**: Comprehensive study package including:
- Standalone interactive HTML study guide (`index.html`)
- Vector publication-ready PDF (`payments_field_guide_breakdown.pdf`)
- Ultra high-resolution summary diagram PNG (`payments_field_guide_breakdown.png`)

---

## What This Package Delivers

1. **Card Money Movement Map (The Four-Party Model)**:
   - Full flow diagram from Cardholder → Merchant → Acquirer/Processor → Card Network (Visa/MC) → Issuing Bank.
   - Stage-by-stage callouts: Real-time Authorization (AVS, CVV, 3DS, soft vs hard declines) and Clearing/Settlement (batching, gross-to-net netting, interchange vs assessment vs acquirer markup).
   - Liability shift and chargeback exposure callouts.

2. **Non-Card Payment Taxonomy**:
   - Four distinct structural models: A2A/Bank Rails (Pix, UPI, Faster Payments, ACH), Delayed/Vouchers (Boleto, OXXO, Konbini), Digital Wallets (Pass-through Apple/Google Pay vs Stored-value PayPal/Alipay), and Crypto/Stablecoins (instant finality on L1/L2s).

3. **Rules, Compliance & Fraud Architecture**:
   - Regulatory frameworks (Durbin amendment caps, EU IFR, PSD2/SCA).
   - Compliance scopes (PCI-DSS tokenization under SAQ-A, KYC/AML, rolling reserves).
   - Fraud mechanics (CNP identity theft, friendly fraud, account takeover, brute-force card testing).

4. **Business Models Funds Flow**:
   - Subscriptions (MIT vs CIT, Card Account Updater, dunning logic).
   - Marketplaces (Merchant of Record vs PayFac, split payouts, money transmission rules).
   - B2B payments (Level 2/3 invoice data interchange reduction, virtual card single-use PANs).

5. **10 Core Principles Strip**:
   - Concise summary of the 10 most critical payment axioms from the book.
